Lidar Maze Explorer

This extension turns an ASCII maze into an explorable first-person lidar game.
Mazes use '.' for open floor, '|' and '_' for walls, and 'E' for the exit. The
string becomes an m×n grid; with Lidar maze mode on you walk with WASD/arrows
and look by clicking the canvas for pointer-lock mouse-look (or the Look slider).

The world stays mostly dark until you Scan (or press Space). Rays march with a
fine continuous step; strip length uses fisheye-corrected perpendicular depth
(distance × cos(ray−view)) so wall heights change smoothly without the usual
bulge at the screen edges. A warm amber perspective floor sits under the
horizon, separate from cyan wall returns. Exit cells ping bright green so they
read as unique identifiers once scanned. Returns accumulate and fade so you
map the maze like a lidar game. ColorLine/Circle still work in normal draw mode.
