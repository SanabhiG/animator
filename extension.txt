Lidar Maze Explorer

This extension turns an ASCII maze into an explorable first-person lidar game.
Mazes are built from three characters: '.' for open floor, and '|' / '_' for
walls. The typed string is parsed into an m×n occupancy grid (padded to a
rectangle). With Lidar maze mode on, you walk the maze with WASD or arrow keys
and look with A/D or the Look slider.

The world stays dark — you cannot see walls by default. Press Space or Scan to
fire a lidar ping: dense rays march with a fine continuous step so hit distance
varies smoothly as you move, and vertical strip lengths scale as wall_scale /
distance rather than jumping between discrete cell sizes. Returns accumulate as
faded cyan stubs so you gradually recognize the layout, like a lidar mapping
game. A brief pulse flash shows live depth strips; afterward only remembered
points remain. Colored geometry (ColorLine / Circle) still works in normal draw
mode.
