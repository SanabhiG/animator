Lidar Maze Explorer

An explorable ASCII maze revealed by lidar scan lines. Mazes use '.' floor,
'|' / '_' walls, and 'E' for a green exit. Toggle lidar mode, then WASD to move,
click the canvas for mouse-look, and click/Space to spray. Each ray paints a
vertical depth-colored line (cyan near → blue far; exits green). Scans accumulate
into permanent memory (localStorage) so rescanning densifies the view. Normal
Gleam draw mode is unchanged.
