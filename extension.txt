Lidar Maze Explorer (Scanner Sombre–style)

Inspired by Scanner Sombre: the maze is pitch dark and you reveal it by spraying
a permanent LIDAR point cloud. Mazes use '.' floor, '|' / '_' walls, and 'E' for
a green exit. WASD moves; click the canvas for mouse-look; click/Space sprays
dots. Each scan adds more speckles (with angular jitter so gaps fill in) —
rescanning the same place makes the cloud denser and the geometry clearer,
rather than replacing or stacking wall columns. Only near-exact duplicates are
skipped. Specks are colored by depth (warm near, cool far); exits stay green.
Memory persists in localStorage. Fisheye-corrected continuous depth drives the
brief live pulse. Normal Gleam draw mode is unchanged.
